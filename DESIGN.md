# Design

## Theme
Light (Warm Parchment)

## Colors
- **Surface**: `oklch(94% 0.01 60)` (Warm Parchment/Paper)
- **Primary**: `oklch(15% 0.01 60)` (Deep Charcoal/Ink)
- **Accent**: `oklch(35% 0.15 25)` (Deep Sacred Crimson)
- **Muted**: `oklch(85% 0.02 60)` (Stone/Clay)

## Typography
- **Display**: Amiri (Sacred Serif)
- **Body**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean Modernity)
- **Monospace**: Courier Prime (Technical/Manuscript Script)

## Components
- **Buttons**: Sharp corners (0px radius), 2px solid border, shadow-offset (neobrutalist but refined).
- **Cards**: Flat, 2px borders, no heavy shadows.
- **Overlays**: Minimalist veils using `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.

## Layout
- **Rhythm**: Generous spacing (minimum 32px between sections).
- **Structure**: Asymmetric balancing of the video preview and the tool parameters.
- **Ornamentation**: Strategic use of Islamic geometric patterns as low-opacity backgrounds (opacity 0.02-0.05).
