# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Devout Muslims and digital content creators who want to share the beauty of the Quran. They seek a tool that feels sacred, intuitive, and high-quality, regardless of their technical skill.

## Product Purpose
To automate the creation of "Wazakkir" (remembrance) style videos. Success is a user feeling a sense of peace while using the tool and pride in the final video output.

## Brand Personality
Sacred, Zen-like, and Minimalist. It should feel like a digital "Mushaf" (manuscript) that has come to life. The "soul of Islam" is reflected through geometric precision, generous whitespace, and a focus on the divine word.

## Anti-references
- SaaS Clichés: No generic blue buttons, heavy shadows, or "startup" illustrations.
- Aggressive Marketing: No popups, urgency-driving banners, or cluttered interfaces.
- Complexity: Avoid overwhelming the user with nested menus or technical jargon.

## Design Principles
- **Geometric Grace**: Use Islamic patterns and ratios to guide layout and spacing.
- **Divine Focus**: The Quranic text is the absolute center of gravity. Everything else serves its legibility and beauty.
- **Atmospheric Calm**: Use a warm, monochromatic palette with deep crimson accents to evoke a sense of tradition and focus.
- **Modern Manuscript**: Blend classical typography (Amiri) with ultra-clean modern layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for Quranic text to ensure readability for all ages.
- Reduced motion options for the background loops.
- Support for various screen sizes (Mobile-first for Reels/TikTok creators).
